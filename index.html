Privacy Policy

JJ Information Technology, Fujairah, United Arab Emirates
App: JJ Company Poster · Last updated: 14/11/2026

1. Who we are

JJ Information Technology ("we", "us") is an IT company in Fujairah, UAE. We use our own internal app, "JJ Company Poster", to publish posts on Facebook and Instagram Pages that we manage – our own Page and the Pages of businesses that have asked us to manage their social media and have added us as a Page admin.

2. What information we use

  • Page access: the Page ID and the Page access token that Facebook gives when a Page admin connects the Page to our app. We never ask for or store anyone's Facebook password.
  • Post content: the text, hashtags and images we prepare for a Page, the time it should be posted, and the link to the published post.
  • Business contact details: the name, email and phone number of the business we work for, so we can tell them when a post is live.

We do not collect information about people who view, like or comment on the posts.

3. How we use it

  • To publish posts on the Page they were written for, at the approved time.
  • To send the Page owner a message with the link to their published post.

A post is only published on the Page it belongs to. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we protect it

Page access tokens are stored encrypted on our own computer and are used only by our app. Only our team can access the app.

5. How long we keep it

We keep Page access and post details while we manage the Page. When a business stops working with us, or asks us to, we delete its Page token and its data.

6. Deleting your data

To remove our access, a Page admin can remove our app in Facebook (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations) or remove JJ Information Technology as a Page admin. To ask us to delete all data we keep about your Page, email us at the address below. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
